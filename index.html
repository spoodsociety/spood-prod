When do items come out of inventory?
Only when the whole order is marked Done. Pieces shown in black (pulled from the shelf) come off the matching Inventory item, once per order. Pieces in red, blue or green were printed fresh for this order and never sat on the shelf, so they don't. Packing and the Ready to Pack / Packing steps leave the counts alone. Undo puts the pieces back.
